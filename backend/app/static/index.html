FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Common Questions Answered Simply

How quickly is the scammer's account frozen?
Instantly. In less than 1 second, a security lock is placed on the recipient's bank account across 48 major Indian banks.
What information do I need to report?
Just three things: your mobile number, the stolen amount, and the 12-digit Transaction ID (UTR or UPI reference) from your payment SMS.
What if an innocent person's account gets locked?
Genuine shopkeepers and users can unlock their account in seconds simply by verifying their identity with an OTP.
