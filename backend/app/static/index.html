CASE LEDGER — 002 — SOVEREIGN MANDATE
About the Initiative

Securing India's Sovereign Digital Economy

DURGAM (दूर्गम) is the specialized national cyber financial fraud defense matrix established under the Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.

Operating in deep coordination with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND), and Department of Telecommunications (DoT), DURGAM solves the critical lag of traditional post-facto cyber investigations by implementing an instant, multi-agency pre-settlement hold switch.
