DURGAM
National Cyber Fraud Protection System
STATUTORY MANDATE

National Cyber Financial Defense Grid

DURGAM is an automated cyber financial protection network connecting 48 commercial banks, state cyber cells, and the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C).

By executing Section 106 BNSS statutory pre-settlement liens within 89 milliseconds, the platform secures defrauded balances in escrow before cashout at physical ATMs.
